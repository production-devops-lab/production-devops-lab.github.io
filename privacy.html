Privacy policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

In short: techtube-uploader is a private tool used only by the ProductionDevOps channel to publish its own videos and reply to comments on its own channel. It does not collect, sell or share personal data.

Who we are

ProductionDevOps is a YouTube channel about real-world DevOps and SRE (youtube.com/@production-devops). Contact: production-devops@proton.me.

What techtube-uploader is

techtube-uploader is the name of the Google OAuth application behind the channel's own publishing tool. It uses the YouTube API Services. It is not offered to the public: the only account that signs in to it is the channel owner's.

What data it accesses

  • The channel's own videos, playlists, captions and thumbnails, to upload and organize them.
  • Public comments posted on the channel's videos (author display name, comment text and date), to read them and post replies from the channel.

It does not access email, contacts, files or any other Google data, and it does not use cookies or tracking on this website.

How the data is used and stored

  • Comments are used only to prepare and post replies. Replies are reviewed by a human before posting and may be drafted with the help of an AI assistant.
  • Comments and the authorization token are stored only on the channel owner's computer. They are not sold, shared, or used for advertising or profiling.
  • Stored comment data is deleted when it is no longer needed to reply, and in any case removed or refreshed at least every 30 days, as the YouTube API Services policies require.

YouTube and Google

This tool uses the YouTube API Services. By interacting with the channel on YouTube you are subject to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google processes data according to the Google Privacy Policy. Access granted to the app can be revoked at any time from the Google security settings.

Your requests

To ask about or request removal of a comment reply or any data related to you, write to production-devops@proton.me.
